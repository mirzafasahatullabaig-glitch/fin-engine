# Business & Finance Reference Glossary (sample)

## Working capital

Working capital is the difference between a company's current assets and its
current liabilities. It measures the short-term liquidity available to fund
day-to-day operations. Positive working capital means current assets exceed
current liabilities.

## Gross profit

Gross profit is revenue minus the cost of goods sold (COGS). It shows how much
a business earns from its core activity before operating expenses, interest and
tax. Gross margin expresses gross profit as a percentage of revenue.

## Operating profit (EBIT)

Operating profit, also called EBIT (earnings before interest and tax), is gross
profit minus operating expenses such as administration, rent, utilities and
marketing. It reflects profitability from normal operations, before financing
costs and income tax.

## Profit before tax (PBT)

Profit before tax is operating profit plus non-operating income, minus finance
costs (such as interest expense) and other non-operating expenses. Crucially,
PBT is not equal to EBIT whenever interest expense exists: the finance cost must
be subtracted. Net profit is then PBT minus income tax expense.

## Cash-flow categories

The statement of cash flows splits movements into three sections. Operating
cash flows arise from the main revenue-producing activities. Investing cash
flows cover the purchase and sale of long-term assets such as machinery.
Financing cash flows cover borrowing, loan repayments, share issues and
dividends. A bank loan received is a financing inflow.
